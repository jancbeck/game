Courtyard game sounds

Footsteps per surface, clicks, one sound per hotspot and the tower door, all from Stable Audio 3 (local, MLX), as listed in game/data/sounds.json. The players play the files at their normalized level, not the in-game level; the walk buttons use the proposed in-game levels. Prompts are in audio/prompts.json under game_sfx.

Proposed levels (levels_db)

categorydBwhy
rain_bed-18.5-2.5 dB from the current -16 dB (the rain loop file is -16.3 LUFS, so the bed plays at about -34.8 LUFS).
fire-6.0Unchanged (3D player at the fire basket).
crows-4.0Unchanged (3D players on the crows).
footsteps-8.0Steps are levelled to -24 dB K-weighted over their first 300 ms, so they play at about -32: 3 dB above the rain bed for the 300 ms of the step, with the transient on top. Clearly heard, never loud. A random +-1.5 dB per step helps against repetition.
click-14.0Clicks measure about -21; they play at about -35, under the rain bed loudness but heard as a transient. Subtle.
hotspots-12.0Files are -16 LUFS; they play at -28, about 7 LU over the rain, as the feedback for an interaction.
doors-12.0Same as hotspots.

Rejected takes

  • Footsteps, first prompt at CFG 3 (t1 and t2 of every surface): mud and straw were hiss (centroid 6 to 8 kHz, 60 to 86 % above 4 kHz); the stone stair was a boom like wood (55 % of the energy at 150 to 600 Hz) with 168 to 438 clipped samples; the other surfaces had 39 to 127 clipped samples and steps running into each other. Replaced by a second prompt with 'a short pause after each step' at CFG 2 (t3 to t6).
  • Footstep candidates from t3 to t6: 548 onsets, 56 kept. Rejected: 264 overlap the previous step (less than 10 dB between the step body and the 80 ms before it), 68 still sounding when the next step starts, 34 faint scuffs (more than 8 dB under the surface steps), 24 clipped, 4 stomps (6 dB over), 8 at the very start of a take (attack may be cut); the rest were spare steps further from the surface's timbre.
  • Walk click t1 to t3: double taps or 27 clipped samples. Interact click t1, t3, t4: second hit 0.25 s after the first, or a long ring.
  • Stable t3 and t4 (second prompt): a low rumble (centroid 230 Hz, nothing above 4 kHz); the local audio model heard a cat. The first-prompt takes stay (the model heard a horse in t1).
  • Well t1 and t2 (CFG 3): 176 and 78 clipped samples. Well t5: sustained 220 to 240 Hz tone (a squeal); the audio model heard meowing.
  • Door open t1 and t2, door close t2 and t3: no clear latch transient in the right place, or 366 clipped samples (close t3).
  • No take has voices or music: no sustained tones outside bell, anvil and creaks, and the local audio model (Audio Flamingo Next) reported no speech, singing or music in any final file.
